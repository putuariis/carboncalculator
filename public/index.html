Metodologi & Faktor Emisi EPA (US EPA Hub)

Perhitungan dalam kalkulator ini sepenuhnya menggunakan standar dari US EPA (Environmental Protection Agency) Center for Corporate Climate Leadership & WARM (Waste Reduction Model v15):

1. Pengomposan Sampah Organik / Food Waste (TKP #1)

Faktor Reduksi EPA: 0.55 kg CO₂e / kg sampah organik.

Referensi: US EPA Waste Reduction Model (WARM v15) - Composting Food Waste versus Landfilling Baseline (-0.50 s/d -0.55 MT CO₂e / short ton teralihkan).

2. Pembuatan Biowash / Organics Valorization (TKP #2)

Faktor Reduksi EPA: 0.61 kg CO₂e / kg sisa kulit buah & sayur.

Referensi: US EPA Organics Diversion & Surfactant Replacement Factor (Life Cycle Assessment of Fruit Waste Valorization).

3. Upcycling & Daur Ulang Plastik LDPE (TKP #3)

Faktor Reduksi EPA: 2.8 kg CO₂e / kg plastik (~0.0245 kg CO₂e / lembar kresek 13.5 gram).

Referensi: US EPA - LDPE / Mixed Plastic Recycling Offset (-1.64 MT CO₂e / short ton virgin resin offset).

4. Emisi Transportasi & Energi Listrik (TKP #4)

Faktor Emisi EPA GHG Center (2024):

  • Sepeda Motor Bensin: 0.111 kg CO₂e / km (EPA Passenger Motorcycle Factor)
  • Mobil Passenger Bensin: 0.205 kg CO₂e / km (EPA Passenger Gasoline Car Factor)
  • Transit Bus / Angkot: 0.055 kg CO₂e / passenger-km (EPA Transit Bus Factor)
  • KRL / Commuter Rail: 0.075 kg CO₂e / passenger-km (EPA Commuter Rail Factor)
  • Mobil / Motor Listrik (EV): 0.052 kg CO₂e / km (EPA EV Grid Average)
  • Konsumsi Listrik Rumah: 0.85 kg CO₂e / kWh (Subgrid PLN / EPA eGRID baseline)
